# NORDLYS Food Plan — 16 Days · 4 Mann · 2 Burners

**Stand: 17.08.2026** · Reise 24.08.–08.09.2026 · Matched against `src/data/itinerary.json` (Stand 17.08.2026).

**Setup:** 2 gas burners, **passive cooler + ice/ice packs** (no 12 V box), no roof box. Day 1 hotel Hamburg, Day 2 bungalow Stockholm, then wild camping with 3 campsite nights. Alcohol/customs is handled in `research/findings.md`, not here.

**The one rule that saves you hundreds of francs:** Norway food is ≈ +30 % vs. Germany. But **Sweden is only +5 %** — so the split is not "buy everything in Hamburg", it's **buy everything before Narvik**. Spreading the bulk shop over Hamburg (Day 1) and Malmö (Day 2) costs almost nothing and keeps the car loadable.

**The second rule — passive cooler:** every fresh-meat meal below is cooked **same day or next day** after its shop. Nothing else works without a compressor box.

**The third rule — no fire:** Norway's open-fire ban covers 15.04.–15.09., i.e. your entire Norway leg. Sweden may have local `eldningsförbud`, and the Day 2 site is in a nature reserve. Every meal here works **in the pan**; a fire is a bonus if you find a designated grill spot (realistically: Days 3, 7, 9, 12).

### Carry-in night — Day 8, Kvalvika

The only night with no car and no cooler. You hike up Ryten with tent gear, sleep on the beach, and walk out before dawn.

- **Carry up:** couscous 400 g, 1 tin tuna, salami end, sun-dried tomatoes, stock cubes, olive oil in a small bottle, 2 emergency instant noodles.
- **Water:** carry it. Don't count on a stream at the beach.
- **Day 9 breakfast is also carry-in:** bars + Knäckebrot, or oats eaten cold. Real breakfast happens in Reine at 09:30.
- **Gas:** one small cartridge is plenty — boiling water only.

> **Timing flag (itinerary, not food):** Day 9 starts at Reinebringen 06:30, but you wake up on Kvalvika beach — that's a 1–1.5 h walk out plus a 30 min drive, so a ~04:30 departure. Worth a look when you next touch the itinerary.

### Breakfasts (rotate)

- **A — Porridge:** oats + UHT milk, honey, raisins, banana. One pot, hot, cheap.
- **B — Bread day:** bread/Knäckebrot + butter, cheese, salami, jam, Nutella, peanut butter.

**Fixed exceptions:** Day 2 hotel buffet (07:00) · Day 9 carry-in, then second breakfast in Reine · **Day 10 the ferry leaves at 07:00** — eat cold in the queue at 06:15 or on board · Day 16 rest area.

### Lunches (cold, from the cooler, at rest stops — ~13 of them)

Sandwiches/wraps with salami, hard cheese, tuna-mayo, cucumber, tomato, mustard · Knäckebrot + mackerel in tomato sauce · cup soups or instant noodles as a hot backup on cold days · fruit, nuts, chocolate.

### Reserve meals (keep 2–3 aboard)

Instant noodles + egg · tortellini + pesto + parmesan · mac & cheese · tomato soup + pan-toasted cheese sandwiches. For rain, exhaustion, or a shop that turns out to be shut. Lofoten gets 15+ rain days a month — you will use at least one.

## Cooler Rules (passive + ice — this is all you've got)

1. **Fresh raw meat = same-day or next-day, no exceptions.** Every meat meal above is built that way; don't improvise a third day.
2. Cured stuff (chorizo, kabanos, salami, hard cheese, vacuum bacon) is the backbone — it survives a week-plus.
3. **Buy bagged ice at every shop.** Refreezing ice packs only works at Moskenes (Day 9) and Molde (Day 12) — Uttakleiv is a tent meadow with no freezer.
4. Frozen meat straight from the shop doubles as an ice pack and buys you a day.
5. Don't waste cooler space on eggs, butter, unopened UHT milk or veg. The cooler is for **raw meat, fish and opened dairy** only.
6. Park in shade, open it as rarely as possible, keep it in the footwell — not the sun-baked boot.

## Two-Burner Workflow
- Burner 1: main pot/pan. Burner 2: rice/potatoes/water for coffee and dishes.
- One-burner nights (gnocchi, jambalaya, couscous, instant): good for wind.
- Windscreen or a foil wall — wind roughly doubles gas use at 68° N.
